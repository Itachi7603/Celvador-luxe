Shipping & Exchange Policy

Dispatch Protocol: Every jewellery item undergoes standard visual quality checks prior to dispatch. Once approved, items are sealed in tamper-evident protective transit packaging to ensure safe delivery.
Domestic Shipping & Timelines
Orders are processed and handed over to express courier partners within 24–48 hours. Standard pan-India delivery arrives within 5 to 7 working days from order confirmation depending on regional courier serviceability.
Free Shipping & Cash On Delivery
We provide Free Delivery on orders across all major serviceable Indian pincodes. Cash on Delivery (COD) is supported across serviceable areas. In case of high-value shipments, recipient verification may be required by courier personnel.
Direct Replacement / Exchange Eligibility
Exchange requests must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery and are strictly eligible for supplier or transit errors:
Return Reason Category Resolution
Defective / Broken / Damaged Product Free replacement dispatched upon claim verification
Wrong Product / Color Delivered Reverse pickup arranged & correct product shipped
Incomplete Product / Missing Parts (Earrings/Tikka) Missing pieces dispatched promptly
If an exchange cannot be fulfilled due to stock unavailability, the request will be converted into a return and refund will be processed in accordance with this policy. Once an item has been exchanged, no further exchanges will be permitted on that order.
Mandatory Unboxing Video Requirement
In line with standard e-commerce anti-fraud guidelines, any claim regarding transit damage, broken stones, wrong item, or missing pieces must be supported by an uninterrupted, uncut and clear unboxing video recorded from the sealed outer parcel. Without verifiable video evidence, claims cannot be processed.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Order Cancellation Window
Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to fulfillment and dispatch. Once tracking details are generated and the shipment is handed over to our delivery partner, in-transit cancellation is not supported.
How Refunds Are Processed
Once the returned product is collected by our logistics partner and passes standard quality inspection (must be in original unused condition with tags and packaging intact), refunds will be initiated:
Refund Method Refund Time Frame
Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI 3 to 7 Business Days (credited to original source account)
Cash on Delivery (COD Orders) 3 to 7 Business Days after customer updates bank/UPI details

Jewellery Care & Return Guidelines

Common Guidelines for Return & Exchange
Prior to initiating any return or exchange, ensure the following conditions are met:
  • The product must be in its original condition with complete brand packaging, tags, pouches, and accessories intact.
  • Must be completely unused, unwashed, and unsoiled with no signs of wear, makeup, or perfume exposure.
  • Must not be damaged or altered while in customer possession.
  • Must match serial/article records of the original shipment.
  • At pickup, delivery agents may conduct a surface quality check; if criteria are failed, pickup and refund will not be accepted.
Artisanal Fashion Jewellery Care
1. Protect from Moisture & Chemicals
Keep away from direct water, alcohol-based perfumes, hair sprays, and sanitizers to maintain long-lasting surface polish and stone lustre.
2. Post-Wear Cleaning
Gently wipe pieces with a soft cotton cloth after wearing to remove sweat or surface dust before storing.
3. Dry Storage
Always store flat in air-tight plastic zip pouches or inside the keepsake box away from moisture.
